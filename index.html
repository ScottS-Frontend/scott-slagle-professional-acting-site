About Scott

For four decades, Scott Slagle has made his home on Northern California stages — and, more recently, in front of cameras and behind microphones. He's the character actor a director calls when a show needs a scheming baron, a soft-hearted father, or a scoundrel with a heart of gold, and the singer who can anchor an ensemble in a first tenor line without ever stealing focus from the story.

Scott has been recognized many times by the local Arty Awards — the region's answer to the Tony Awards — and took home the win in 1989.

  • 1986 Best Lead Actor in a Comedy — Nominee
    Brighton Beach Memoirs as Eugene M. Jerome
  • 1989 Favorite Featured Actor in a Drama — Winner
    Come Back to the 5 & Dime, Jimmy Dean, Jimmy Dean as Joe
  • 2012 Best Supporting Actor in a Comedy — Nominee
    Arsenic & Old Lace as Teddy Brewster
  • 2013 Best Supporting Actor in a Musical — Nominee
